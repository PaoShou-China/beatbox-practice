# Real human Beatbox sample credits

The default voice pack contains 10 real human recordings, two per sound. All assets are served by this repository; there is no voice synthesis in this pack. The separate synthesized comparison pack is optional.

## PF / outward K — Spencer X

Author: Spencer X. Work: **One Beatboxer, 20 Beatbox Snares**, 24 June 2018.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:One_Beatboxer,_20_Beatbox_Snares.webm

Original video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JU13fPNymnU

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The source video directly labels its Outward K and Pf demonstration sections. These samples replace the earlier unspecified snare reference; K is outward, matching the site's beginner instructions.

Excerpt intervals in seconds of the Commons source:

- K 1: 55.000–55.160
- K 2: 55.220–55.360
- PF 1: 71.000–71.700
- PF 2: 72.175–72.335

## O / open hi-hat — Pepouni

Performer: Pepouni. Dataset: **Beatboxset1**, collected in 2008 by Dan Stowell, Queen Mary University of London, from Humanbeatbox.com contributors with their permission.

Source: https://archive.org/details/beatboxset1

Original file: https://archive.org/download/beatboxset1/putfile_pepouni.wav

License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Two independent annotators, Helena du Toit and Diako Rasoul, both label these events `ho` (open hi-hat). Exact selected intervals:

- O 1: 1.873560–2.105 seconds
- O 2: 4.852970–5.045 seconds

Annotation files: `Annotations_HT/putfile_pepouni.csv` and `Annotations_DR/putfile_pepouni.csv` in the dataset. Annotation definitions and licensing: https://archive.org/download/beatboxset1/about_beatboxset1.txt

## Changes and reuse

Changes by Beatbox Practice: cropped individual hits, mixed excerpts to mono, applied 1 ms entry / 8 ms exit fades, and encoded the new excerpts as 192 kbps MP3. Playback trims near-silent edges and balances peak levels with gain capped at 4×. No pitch or speed transformation is applied to the sample pack. B/T use the existing HQ previews with playback edge trimming and level balance.

The authors do not endorse this site. Retain the relevant author, work/source link, license link, and modification notice when reusing samples. The Pepouni O excerpts and adaptations containing them are distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0; this share-alike condition applies to adapted audio, not automatically to unrelated application code. Human WAV exports include these credits as a RIFF INFO comment. Keep attribution and the applicable audio license when distributing exported adaptations containing O.

`ipa-kit.json` retains its filename for compatibility, but its version 2 bank contains recordings from all three credited sources and records each sample's license separately.
